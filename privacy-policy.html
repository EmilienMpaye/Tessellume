Privacy Policy

Tessellume Puzzle · EmlHappyGames · Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Tessellume Puzzle ("the app"), published by EmlHappyGames ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. The app is a single-player block puzzle game. It has no user accounts, no chat and no sign-in.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, unlocked items, settings such as sound and vibration) is saved only on your device. We do not upload it to our own servers, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one) and a banner ad in the shop. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID),
  • general device and app information (for example device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • interactions with ads (for example views and taps).

Depending on your region, ads may be personalised based on this information. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are used, through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app (shown where applicable). You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase was made, so the app can unlock it, including when you reinstall the app.

4. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app does not use your camera or microphone.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is kept according to Google's own policies; you can manage it in your Google account and Android device settings.

7. Security

Communication between the app and Google's services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
